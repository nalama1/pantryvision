# PantryVision

## What It Is

A web application for household inventory management (food, cleaning supplies, office supplies). The user uploads a photo of a product, a vision AI model (Amazon Bedrock) extracts the name, brand, presentation, and expiration date, the user confirms the data, and the system saves it to a personal inventory with expiration and low-stock alerts.

## Target Audience

People responsible for managing household supplies — whether they buy in bulk or retail — who need expiration and restocking control without keeping manual records.

## Problem It Solves

There is no record of what was purchased, when, and when it expires. This leads to waste from expired products and duplicate or late purchases.

## Key Objectives

- Minimize manual data entry (use photos + AI)
- Alert 7 days in advance before something runs out or expires
- Differentiate stock by "pack" and "loose unit"
- Keep personal data secure (product images/invoices)
